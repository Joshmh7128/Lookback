Hover the timeline to read values. Click to seek.

Each lane is scaled to its own range. Audio lanes show the maximum per 100 ms. The mel lane is a log-mel spectrogram, low frequencies at the bottom. Rule lanes show each condition's margin over its threshold: above the dashed zero line the condition holds. The profile lane shows which game, sub-game or mode was active; the candidates lane shows the clips built from the events (darker is a higher score). In the events lane, the tick is the moment, the band is the clip window and the height is the confidence; hover for the evidence.
